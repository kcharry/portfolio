MathML Extra credit

Einstein's mass-energy equivalence formula:

E = m c 2

MathML has been part of HTML for a long time, but browser support used to be inconsistent because different browsers added support at different times. This made it harder for developers to make sure math content worked across all browsers. Now, with better standardization and improved browser support, MathML is more widely supported, though some older browsers may still have issues.
